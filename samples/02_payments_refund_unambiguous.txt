Refunds are charged twice when the customer retries the refund button

1. Open an order with a completed payment
2. Press "Refund" and wait for the spinner
3. Press "Refund" a second time before the page updates

Expected: one refund.
Actual: two refunds are issued against the same charge. The payments ledger
shows two entries with different ids for one order. Reproduced on staging four
times out of four.
